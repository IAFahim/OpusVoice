OpusVoice QR Console

What is this?

The web twin of the Android app's QR system: scan a code, get typed data.

  • pinhole1:… — a Pinhole connection-string ticket
  • udp://host:port — a plain RTP target
  • anything else is shown as raw text — nothing is silently lost

Scanning runs entirely on-device: the camera feed never leaves this page. Installable as a PWA.
